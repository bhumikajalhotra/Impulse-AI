🛍️ Impulse.ai
Your bank account's aggressively honest best friend.
Impulse.ai is a "brutalist" shopping assistant designed to add intentional friction to your online shopping. Built for the Anakin.io x Google Gemini Hackathon, it uses high-end AI to tell you exactly why you should (or absolutely should not) hit that "Buy Now" button.

💅 The Concept
In a world of one-click checkouts, Impulse.ai is the reality check. We take any product URL and run it through a dual-personality AI engine to give you:

Girl Math Justification: The "logical" reason why that ₹5,000 purchase is actually an investment.

Savage Reality Check: The brutal truth about your spending habits.

🚀 Key Features
🕵️‍♂️ Universal Scraper (Powered by Anakin.io): Bypasses the complex anti-bot shields of sites like Amazon and Myntra to pull real-time product data.

🧠 Personality Toggles: * Savage Mode: Brutal honesty for when you need to save money.

Enabler Mode: Finds every "Girl Math" reason to validate your spree.

✨ Bento-Style Dashboard: A sleek, glassmorphic UI that makes financial judgment look like a premium experience.

🤳 Share My Shame: Instantly copy your AI verdict to roast yourself in the group chat.

🛠️ The Secret Sauce
The Brain: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (Fast, witty, and incredibly smart).

The Muscle: Anakin.io Scraper API (The heavy lifter for data extraction).

The Look: React 19 + Tailwind CSS v4 + Framer Motion.

The Engine: Node.js backend deployed on Render.

📖 How to Use
Paste any product URL from Amazon, Myntra, or boAt.

Select your vibe (Savage or Enabler).

Analyze and watch the AI judge your life choices in real-time.

Share the verdict to your friends.
